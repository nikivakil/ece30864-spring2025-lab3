1. What are some pros and cons of Agile development (vs non-Agile)?
    Pros:
    - Uses an iterative approach
    - Constant collaboration with customers
    Cons:
    - Requirements are always changing
    - Not suitable for small teams

2. Explain the roles and responsibilities of the Scrum Master vs the Product Owner.
    Scrum Master: All scrum related tasks - JIRA tooling, facilitating sprints and meetings, standups.
    Product Owner: Sets direction for the team, handles release management and stakeholder management.


3. What are the three types of meetings that happen during a sprint cycle?
    1. Sprint Planning meeting
        - Determine what to complete in the upcoming Sprint
        - Move stories in sprint scope from backlog to current sprint
        - Determine story points and assign stories to team

    2. Daily Scrum/Standup meeting
        - Short meeting that happens every day of the Sprint
        - Team members discuss work completed, work plan for the day, and any blockers

    3. Sprint Retrospective meeting
        - Occurs at the end of the sprint
        - Discuss work complete and move incomplete work to backlog
        - Close sprint

4. Differentiate between Epic, Story, Bug, and Task issue types in JIRA.
    Epic: Captures a large body of work that can be broken down into smaller stories
    Story: A software system requirement that is expressed in a few short sentences
    Bug: An issue with one of the stories that needs to be fixed
    Task: A smaller unit of work that goes within a story
